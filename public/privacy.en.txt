3. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS AND DATA PROCESSED

In the course of using the `Countdown FC - Matches` application, the
following categories of personal data are processed.

3.1 Athletes and event registrants

- Identification data: first name, last name
- Tax code (codice fiscale)
- Date of birth and, derived from it, age and minor/adult status
- Gender
- Contact data: email address, phone number (optional)
- Club or team affiliation (optional)
- Registration data: tournament, discipline, weight category, weight recorded
  at weigh-in, payment status, attendance at the event, notes entered by the
  organizing staff
- Sporting data: matches fought, opponent, assigned corner, outcome, round and
  method of ending the match (including KO, technical KO and disqualification),
  scores assigned by judges, total number of matches per discipline
- Temporary verification code (OTP) sent by email during online registration,
  stored exclusively as a hash and for 10 minutes

3.2 Administrator users (Controller's staff)

- Username and email address
- Password, stored exclusively as a hash (bcrypt)
- Technical session data: IP address, browser user agent, date and time of
  access and last activity
- Application access token

3.3 Common technical data

- Browsing and log data: IP address, browser type, requests made, date and
  time of access

The tax code and date of birth are processed as they are necessary to
uniquely identify the data subject, to verify age, and to assign the subject
to the categories set out by sporting regulations.

Special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR): the Controller does not request and
does not intend to process data concerning health, physical condition, or
medical fitness through the application. Organizing staff are instructed not
to enter health-related information in the notes fields. The weight recorded
and the method of ending the match are processed solely as technical-sporting
data necessary to carry out the competition.

4. PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

The personal data collected are processed for the following purposes:

a) managing registrations for sporting events organized by the Controller,
   including generating the registration document in PDF format;
b) verifying the identity and email address of the registrant by sending a
   temporary code (OTP);
c) organizing and running the competitions: matching athletes by category,
   discipline, weight and experience, managing the bracket/board and
   recording outcomes;
d) publishing the board and match results (see point 8);
e) managing the internal administrative application and staff accounts;
f) internal reporting and statistical analysis;
g) fulfilling legal, accounting, tax and insurance obligations connected with
   organizing the events;
h) service communications relating to the registration and the event.

5. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

Each purpose is based on a specific legal basis:

- Purposes a), b), c), e), h): performance of a contract or of pre-contractual
  measures requested by the data subject, pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  Registering for the event and participating in the competition constitute
  the relationship by virtue of which the data are necessary.
- Purposes d) and f): legitimate interest of the Controller, pursuant to Art.
  6(1)(f) GDPR, in documenting and making the running of the competitions
  available for consultation, and in assessing the progress of its sporting
  activity. The data subject has the right to object to this processing
  (see point 12).
- Purpose g): compliance with legal obligations, pursuant to Art. 6(1)(c)
  GDPR.

At the time of online registration, the data subject is required to
acknowledge having read this policy. Where processing is based on consent,
the data subject may withdraw it at any time by writing to the Controller,
without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the
withdrawal.

6. SOURCE OF THE DATA

Data are collected:

- directly from the data subject, through the online registration form;
- from third parties, when the registration or data entry is carried out by
  the athlete's sporting club, their coach, the parent or guardian of a
  minor, or the Controller's organizing staff based on information provided
  at the event;
- automatically while using the application, only for the technical and log
  data indicated in point 3.3.

7. PROCESSING METHODS AND SECURITY MEASURES

Processing is carried out using IT tools and, residually, paper records, with
logic strictly related to the stated purposes and in a manner that ensures
the security and confidentiality of the data, adopting technical and
organizational measures appropriate under Art. 32 GDPR, including:

- transmission of data over an encrypted channel (HTTPS/TLS);
- access to the administrative area restricted to authorized personnel only,
  subject to individual authentication with a password stored as a hash;
- verification of the email address via a temporary code for online
  registration, with a limit on the number of attempts and requests;
- rate limiting on public and authentication endpoints;
- security headers and a Content Security Policy on the web application;
- periodic encrypted backups;
- exclusion of the application from search engine indexing.

8. PUBLICATION OF RESULTS AND THE BOARD

For purpose d) in point 4, the application provides a public page,
accessible without authentication, on which the following are visible:

- first and last name of the athletes involved in the matches;
- club or team affiliation;
- discipline and weight category;
- time, status and outcome of the match, method of ending it, indication of
  the winner and judges' scores.

This publication also concerns athletes who are minors. The following are
not published: tax code, date of birth, email, phone number, weight
recorded, notes, and payment data.

The page is excluded from search engine indexing (noindex) and is protected
by rate limiting. The data subject may object to the publication of their
data by writing to the Controller.

9. RECIPIENTS AND DATA PROCESSORS

Personal data are not disclosed to third parties for their own purposes, nor
communicated for marketing purposes.

Outside of the publication described in point 8, data may be processed, as
data processors pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR and on the basis of specific
agreements, by the following categories of parties:

- providers of web application hosting and distribution services;
- providers of hosting services for the application infrastructure and the
  database;
- the email service provider used to send service communications and
  verification codes;
- the provider of the encrypted backup storage service.

Data may also be communicated to public authorities, sports federations and
promotion bodies, insurance companies and consultants of the Controller, to
the extent necessary to comply with legal or regulatory obligations
connected with organizing the events.

An up-to-date list of data processors is available on request by writing to
the Controller.

No third-party services are used for analytics, marketing, profiling or
tracking purposes, nor are resources loaded from external content delivery
networks.

10. TRANSFER OF DATA ABROAD

Data processing takes place within the European Economic Area (EEA). Should
a provider involve a transfer of data to third countries, the transfer will
take place only where there is an adequacy decision of the European
Commission, or on the basis of the standard contractual clauses referred to
in Art. 46 GDPR, together with any supplementary measures that may be
necessary.

11. RETENTION PERIOD

Data are retained for the time necessary to fulfil the stated purposes and,
in particular:

- identification, contact, registration and sporting data (matches fought,
  outcomes, scores) of athletes: for the duration of the relationship with
  the Controller and, thereafter, up to 5 years from the last participation
  in an event, also for the purpose of matching by experience level in
  subsequent events;
- administrator user accounts: for the duration of their role and up to 12
  months thereafter;
- application log data: up to 12 months;
- session data: 120 minutes of inactivity;
- cookie containing the access token for the restricted area: 7 days;
- password reset token: 60 minutes;
- verification code (OTP) for online registration: 10 minutes;
- encrypted backup copies: 16 days.

Upon expiry, data are deleted or irreversibly anonymized, without prejudice
to any retention obligations set out by applicable law. The data subject may
at any time request early deletion of their data in the cases provided for
by law.

12. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

Pursuant to Articles 15-22 of the GDPR, the data subject has the right to:

- Access their personal data (right of access)
- Obtain rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
- Obtain erasure of data (right to be forgotten), in the cases provided for
  by law
- Obtain restriction of processing
- Obtain data portability
- Object to processing based on legitimate interest, including the
  publication described in point 8
- Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
- Lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority
  (www.garanteprivacy.it)

To exercise these rights, the data subject may contact the Controller at the
email address: countdownroadtoshooto@gmail.com

The Controller will respond without undue delay and, in any case, within one
month of receiving the request, a period which may be extended by a further
two months in cases of particular complexity, with notice given to the data
subject.

13. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING

The application uses automated criteria to propose matches between athletes
(weight category, discipline, gender, age and experience level). These
proposals are always subject to review and modification by the organizing
staff.

No profiling or fully automated decision-making processes that produce legal
effects or similarly significantly affect the data subject are carried out,
within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

14. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE

The application uses exclusively technical cookies and local storage tools,
necessary for the operation of the service. No profiling, third-party or
marketing cookies are used, and consent is therefore not required for them.

In particular:

- `sanctum.token.cookie`: contains the access token for the restricted area;
  it is present only for authenticated users and lasts 7 days;
- `ui-primary-color`: stores the interface color chosen by the user;
- language and theme (light/dark) preference cookies, where set;
- `matches.api-override` (browser local storage): a technical configuration
  parameter used for maintenance purposes.

These data remain in the user's browser and are not transmitted to third
parties.

15. MINORS

The application also processes data of athletes who are minors, as they are
admitted to participate in competitions under the applicable sporting
regulations.

Registration of a minor must be carried out by the parent or the person
exercising parental responsibility, or by the sporting club to which the
minor belongs on their behalf. The parent or guardian makes the declarations
and, where required, gives the consent provided for in this policy on behalf
of the minor, and is responsible for the accuracy of the data provided.

As indicated in point 8, the first name, last name, club, discipline, weight
category and outcome of minors' matches are visible on the public board
page. The parent or guardian may object to such publication by writing to
the Controller.

The Controller does not collect data of minors for purposes other than the
organization and documentation of sporting competitions.

16. NATURE OF THE DATA PROVISION

Providing the data indicated as mandatory is necessary for registration and
participation in the events: failure to provide it will make it impossible
to access the services offered. Providing the data indicated as optional
(phone number, club affiliation) is not necessary, and its absence has no
consequences.

data subjects where the breach poses a high risk, pursuant to Articles 33-34
GDPR.
